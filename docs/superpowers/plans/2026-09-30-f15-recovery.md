## Review focus

- Cancellation cannot abandon an accepted writer operation or advance a history cursor first.
- Missing markers, dirty sessions and unsupported completeness cannot become complete.
- An empty/unfinished history response cannot prove candle completeness.
- Backfill cannot consume the live reserve; metadata and quarantine work stay bounded.
- Book resnapshot restores current state only; the historical gap remains visible.
