## Search record and read depth

Five Consensus searches succeeded: real-time data vintages/revisions; overlapping multi-horizon forecast evaluation; financial temporal aggregation/leakage; Bitcoin/crypto hourly-to-weekly return baselines; and delayed/sequential forecast calibration. Three simultaneous initial searches hit a short rate limit and were retried only where relevant. Results below were individually fetched from Consensus. Read depth is title, metadata and available abstract, not full text. Consensus omitted abstracts for some Bitcoin results. Local Firecrawl search located publisher/author pages; publisher scrapes returned anti-bot errors or empty bodies. No cloud Firecrawl was used. DOI/year discrepancies are identified rather than silently resolved.

## Implications for the registered work

PI02 should make availability and revision evidence explicit, derive endpoints by timestamp, and report eligibility exclusions. The literature above cannot manufacture historical first-seen times. PI03 should compare its frozen zero/mean/ridge baselines on identical chronological origins, account for long-horizon overlap, and keep model or inference additions exploratory unless registered before outcomes are inspected. PI04 should score only matured labels and keep forward monitoring distinct from the protected final period. None of these papers supplies source-use rights or a substitute for the PI02 feasibility gate.
