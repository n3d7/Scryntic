# PI02 execution plan

Status: PI02 execution approved on 2026-10-22. This plan records the PI02 implementation scope; it does not authorize PI03 or PI04. Linear owns task status and dependencies; `ARCHITECTURE.md` and the registered PI00 policy own the scientific and security boundaries. See [PI02 validation](../validation/PI02_VALIDATION.md) for the measured outcome and its limits.

## Objective and fixed boundaries

PI02 must produce a bounded, deterministic v2 path from qualified finalized bars to duration-based targets and an auditable feasibility decision for 1h, 1d, 7d, and 28d. It must preserve the distinction between historical reconstruction and observations whose availability was actually recorded at the time. The registered PI00 policy fixes the Binance Vision BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT 1h historical-reconstruction source scope, hourly origins for 1h, daily UTC origins for longer horizons, last-trade-close price basis, future final reservation, and policy digest. A 28d duration is not a calendar month.

The v1 128-step and 10,000-row limits remain intact. The PI01 default-deny rule for protected raw builds remains intact except for a narrowly verified PI02 development-input admission. Unknown availability, missing constituent bars, unqualified source use, and inaccessible final labels remain fail-closed. No model receives exchange credentials or financial execution authority.

## Work sequence after approval

1. **Freeze the PI02 input and feasibility specification.** Record source frequency and rights scope, origin cadence, target endpoint convention, feature lookback, minimum complete history by horizon, split boundaries, purge/embargo rule, resource ceilings, and policy/recipe versions before fitting is possible. The 1h/1d/7d/28d target durations imply 1/24/168/672 complete hourly intervals from an origin; feature history is a separate requirement. Do not infer point-in-time availability from bar timestamps or a later archive snapshot.
2. **Build pure, bounded v2 bar and endpoint operations.** Resample only complete, finalized constituent intervals on UTC boundaries. Reject gaps, duplicates, conflicting revisions, incomplete bars, and time-quality failures with explicit reasons; never fill or manufacture a bar. For an observed availability plane, derived availability is no earlier than the latest required constituent availability and must respect the chosen as-of cutoff. For historical reconstruction, retain unknown historical first-seen/revision times as unknown. Resolve duration endpoints by timestamps rather than widening v1 step caps.
3. **Admit only qualified development input.** Extend the PI01 protected-input decision for an explicitly identified source, policy, snapshot, development interval, and availability plane. Preserve source-use decisions and raw partition/row lineage. Keep the final interval, label-bearing inputs at forecast issue time, unknown planes, and broader protected raw builds denied. Every admission or refusal must be explainable in the existing access evidence.
4. **Emit a small versioned PI02 output contract.** Make development origin records, eligibility decisions, and matured outcome versions deterministic from policy/recipe/source digests and raw lineage. Keep pre-outcome fields separate from endpoint values. Store or export only the artifacts needed to replay the feasibility decision and allow later consumers to join by stable origin/horizon identity. Do not create a model, scheduler, or forecast ledger.
5. **Run the feasibility gate.** Measure actual eligible and matured candidate counts for each instrument, horizon, and chronological train/validation/test interval after missingness, time-quality, availability, purge, embargo, and source-use exclusions. Report effective nonoverlapping long-horizon support rather than multiplying correlated instruments or treating overlapping windows as independent. Measure peak memory, runtime, and persisted size against declared bounds. Block the affected horizon or evidence class when history or support is insufficient; do not substitute shorter horizons or inaccessible final labels.
6. **Verify and hand off.** Exercise gap, duplicate, late-revision, resampling, UTC boundary, endpoint, maturity, unavailable-at-origin, protected-admission, and v1 regression fixtures. Check determinism from the same input digest and explicit new outcome versions after corrections. Run the task-required local qualification before any PI02 completion claim, and distinguish local results from later CI, SonarQube, Trivy, and Semgrep PR evidence.

## Exit decision and unresolved limits

PI02 is complete only when its deterministic v2 path and guarded development admission pass focused and v1 regression checks, and a versioned feasibility manifest demonstrates which horizons and evidence classes have sufficient permitted history, matured split support, and bounded resource use. A green test suite alone does not qualify an unsupported 28d claim. The registered 273-day exploratory test interval yields at most nine ideal nonoverlapping 28d blocks, while the reserved final interval yields at most 41 before exclusions; the actual gate must calculate support after masks and must never open final labels to make development feasible.

Historical archive first-seen and revision times remain unknown unless independently observed, so retrospective reconstruction cannot be presented as an as-observed forecast record. Bybit and forward-source rights remain separate decisions. If a required choice conflicts with the registered policy or architecture, stop the affected work and request the corresponding decision before implementation proceeds.
